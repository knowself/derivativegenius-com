Mike Stewart simplifies the whole internet to two jobs:

Chapter 3 · Weapon A

The jingle that sticks in their head

Every business has a 2–4 word phrase that puts them #1 — e.g. "Nashville Party Boat Rental," "Pink Plumber," "Mount Lawley Pest Control." Problem: locals don't know to type it. Solution: plant it with melody.

The 21-time rule

"When you hear marketing information set to melody 20 times, on the 21st time you will never get it out of your subconscious for the rest of your life."

Proof: 30 years ago in Smyrna, Georgia, The Pink Plumber ran: "Google Pink, click the link, or call 404-222-PINK." Listeners still recite it decades later. Same mechanism as 800-588-2300 Empire Today.

The 15-second recipe

  1. 0–5s · The anchor: sing the exact search term + who you are. Must land before "Skip" appears.
  2. 5–12s · The benefit: plain-spoken promise in owner voice.
  3. 12–15s · The nudge: "Search [term] today!" or phone.
Golden rule — don't "help" with lyrics. Owners love slogans and rhymes that kill the earworm. Mike's line: "We write the lyrics, we pick the music, we follow the formula. If you don't like the free AI version, we'll do a $2,000 studio version however you want." Nobody ever pays the $2,000.

Chapter 4 · Weapon B

The YouTube loophole (legal, built into Google's billing)

Over half of local YouTube now plays on living-room Smart TVs. Skippable ads force 5 seconds of watching — and Google charges $0 if they skip before 30 seconds.

0–5s (mandatory, free)6–30s (skippable)
Jingle starts instantly. Brand + search term sung. 100% hear it.Problem, proof, offer. Pay only if watched to 30s or clicked.

The living-room effect: kids watching Bluey, remote on the coffee table — nobody dives to skip. Even at 70–80% skip rates, everyone heard you.

Abilene, Texas pest control: 8 million skips (free saturation) + 3 million full views on ~$200–$400/mo ad spend. Revenue tripled and quadrupled. Families hummed it daily.

Targeting: geography beats guesswork

Don't micro-filter age, interests, parental status. Draw a tight 15–20 mile radius / zip cluster and let Google's machine find buyers.

Chapter 5 · Weapon C

The page that rings: one problem, one video, one button

Mike's founding discovery: "People will watch before they read." Replace bloated 20-page sites with one focused Video Sales Letter per urgent problem.

The 5-part page

  1. Headline with risk reversal — see formula below
  2. 45–60 sec video + jingle — solve the exact search, human face
  3. Proof strip — Google 5-stars / badges right under video
  4. Plain-spoken copy — agitate the pain, answer FAQs
  5. Sticky tap-to-call — always in the mobile thumb zone

The headline that has worked 15 years

"We will give you the most thorough, amazing, unbelievable [service] guaranteed or it's free."

HVAC: "We will make your house ice cold or it's free." Plumbing: "We will fix your leaky toilet today guaranteed or it's free." Roofing: "Your roof will not leak, guaranteed or it's free." Anxious homeowners just want safety. Almost nobody claims — they just call.

Ads that feed it: Problem → Agitate → "Watch my video"

Dan Kennedy style: "Tired of termites? Scared they're eating your home? Want them gone today? Watch my video." Adding "Watch my video" quadrupled click-through — it promises clarity, not homework.

Chapter 6 · Save your money

Two traps that eat local budgets

Trap 1 · The award-winning website. Giant sliders, stock video, clever slogans ("Excellence in Every Pipe") push your number below the fold. Result: ~90% bounce in 10 seconds. An award is often a negative indicator of calls. And never hide "call" behind a hamburger menu — Mike: "Ask a baby boomer what a hamburger menu is — they think of In-N-Out."
Trap 2 · The social-media walled garden. Dopamine scrolling isn't emergency intent — nobody with a burst pipe opens Instagram. Worse, Facebook/Instagram hide posts from Google and AI crawlers. Posts on your domain compound for years; social posts evaporate.

Chapter 8 · What to do Monday

Your next step: a 20-minute audit

Small owners "don't know what they don't know" — and get spammed by faceless agencies. The fix is a short, in-person diagnostic:

  1. Google your money term — are you in the Map Pack?
  2. Open your site on your phone — can you call in one tap without scrolling or menus?
  3. Count reviews — under 20, unanswered 1-stars, no owner replies?
  4. Search your blog — zero indexed answers on your own domain?
If you failed 2 or more: get the audit. Bring one problem ("termites," "leaky roof," "slow season"). Leave with one page, one video outline, and one jingle lyric to test. Run the 5-minute self-audit ↓

Chapter 9 · Weapon D

How content gets made (without you writing)

"It's a shorter distance from your brain to your tongue than to your hands." Owners freeze at keyboards but can't stop talking when asked the right technical question. So never start with "write a blog." Start with talk, then transcribe.

The 5-step extraction

  1. Name 50 problems you solve. Not "we kill bugs" — list them: subterranean termites, drywood termites, bedbugs, German roaches, brown recluse, roof rats…
  2. Pick one diagnostic question: "What are subterranean termites, and why are they dangerous to a foundation?"
  3. Record 3 minutes. Phone voice memo is fine — explain it like to a neighbor.
  4. Transcribe to your domain. Full text = blog post + FAQ schema. Audio stays embedded.
  5. Syndicate the audio. One RSS feed → Apple, Spotify, Amazon, YouTube = free authoritative backlinks.
50-problems worksheet (15 min): draw 5 columns × 10 rows. Columns: Pests / Complaints / Seasons / Prices asked / Jobs you love. Fill 50 cells. Circle the 4 most-asked — that's this month's content.
Recorder checklist: quiet truck/office · phone 6 inches away · 3 min max · state name + town + problem first · end with "Search [SERP term] or tap to call."
Transcript template: Title = customer question ("How much… in [Town]?") · 150-word answer · 3 bullets · FAQ (3 Qs) · Call box. Publish weekly — compounds in Google + AI answers.

Appendix A · Tear-out

5-minute self-audit

  1. Does your Google Ad (or search result) go to your homepage instead of one problem page?
  2. On mobile, is there a sticky tap-to-call button with no menu hunting?
  3. Does your hero waste the screen on sliders while hiding reviews + phone?
  4. GBP: <20 reviews, unanswered 1-stars, zero owner replies, no fresh photos?
  5. All content trapped on Facebook/Instagram, zero articles/podcasts on your domain?

Score: 0 fails = defend the lead · 1–2 = fix this month · 3+ = you need the full playbook.
